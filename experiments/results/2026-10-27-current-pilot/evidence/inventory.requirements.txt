For nonnegative built-in int stock and requested where requested <= stock, inventory.remaining(stock, requested) returns the integer stock - requested, including zero when requested equals stock.
For requested > stock with both arguments nonnegative built-in ints, inventory.remaining raises ValueError.
For a negative built-in int stock or requested, inventory.remaining raises ValueError.
If either argument is not a built-in int, including bool, inventory.remaining raises TypeError.
The system shall quickly notify users when stock is low.
