# Reusable controlled recovery fixtures

These development fixtures retain a deliberately bad import and a valid boundary
failure, plus a type-complete adaptive follow-up. They support scripted regression
and optional controlled shared-preparation modes. Source projects, contracts and
witnesses remain unchanged. They are not current paid model observations or
independent semantic labels.

The old recovery and retention studies and their dedicated manifests were removed
as whole studies at the owner's request. [Cleanup](../../experiments/cleanup-v22.json)
records their Git-history revision. `typed-followup.provenance.json` retains the
original parent archive hash and candidate provenance; the retired parent can be
recovered from that revision. No original outcome is relabeled or pooled with v22.

Current independent paid results are in the [experiment index](../../experiments/README.md).
Admission-blocked candidates do not constitute runtime failures, and admission
correction does not establish admitted-runtime test-error repair effectiveness.
